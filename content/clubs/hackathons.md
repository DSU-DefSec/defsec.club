# Hackathon Club

## About Us

Welcome to Hackathon Club! Our mission is to get more DSU students building and shipping real projects at competitive hackathons.

Hackathons are 24–48 hour build sprints where teams turn an idea into a working project and demo it to judges. If you like working on real projects, why not take a shot at winning some cash for it? Worst that could happen if your project doesn't win? You've got a new project on your resume! All levels of experience, from complete beginner to expert, are welcome to drop in anytime! We'd love to have you come build with us!

We will be going on various hackathons over the course of each semester, which will be announced on the Computer Club discord as they come by. Feel free to hit up the [#hackathons](https://discord.com/channels/673707857337647134/1470795703964930151) channel if you're ever looking for a teammate!

We meet bi-weekly to discuss hackathon strategies, share helpful tips, and explore interesting ideas and discoveries from hackathons.
