# Competitive Programming Club

## About Us

We study algorithms and data structures and compete in the [International Collegiate Programming Contest (ICPC)](/competitions/external_competitions/icpc/). We talk about problem solving techniques specifically for competitive programming and algorithmic problem-solving.

Beginners welcome, but we may be talking about more advanced topics; I always try to include some separate options for beginners for practice sessions.

## Meeting Times

Normal Computer Club meeting times: an occasional Tuesday from 6-7 pm CST

We also hold a weekly practice session on Fridays from 7-8 pm where we drill questions, talk about methods, and solve problems together!

## Practice Resources

- [Kattis](https://open.kattis.com)
  - Our default practice platform. Nearly every past ICPC question is on here. Supports creating contests, practicing past problemsets, and a range of languages.
- [Codeforces](https://codeforces.com)
  - Generally a different sort of question; often much more math-based, will frequently require more "trick"ish answers to solve things. Great site if you want to get practice in live contests as they regularly hold online contests with new questions.
- [CSES](https://cses.fi)
  - More straightforward practice. Questions are separated into topics and ordered within them by difficulty. 
- [AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp)
  - Also great for live contests. More difficult to find a pool of problems to work on; their problem archive is not open on the site (you just have to go back through past contests). Japanese site, so the content renders weirdly sometimes.
- [USACO Guide](https://usaco.guide)
  - A study guide made for the USA Computing Olympiad--a high school level contest. Of course, we don't participate in this, but the content is very helpful and applicable.
