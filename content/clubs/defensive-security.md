# Defensive Security (DefSec) Topic

## About Us

The Defensive Security Club is a vibrant community of cybersecurity enthusiasts, students, and experts who are passionate about protecting digital assets and securing the online world. Our mission is to promote cybersecurity awareness, provide educational resources, and create a collaborative environment for students interested in defensive security.

## What You'll Find In Our Club

- CCDC Prep: Learn everything you'll need to know to excel at [CCDC](/competitions/external_competitions/ccdc/) and join the team!
- Security Fundamentals: Dive into the basics of cybersecurity, from understanding threats and vulnerabilities to exploring security best practices.
- Tools and Techniques: Discover the latest tools and techniques used in defensive security, including network security, threat detection, and incident response.
- Tutorials and How-Tos: Step-by-step guides and tutorials on various security topics to help you sharpen your skills.
- News and Updates: Stay up-to-date with the latest cybersecurity news, trends, and events.

## Get Involved

Are you passionate about cybersecurity and interested in joining our club? We welcome students of all skill levels. Whether you're a seasoned professional or a beginner, there's a place for you here. Participate in our meetings, workshops, and events to expand your knowledge and network with like-minded individuals.

Thank you for visiting the Defensive Security Club Wiki. We hope you find the information here valuable and that it helps you on your journey to becoming a cybersecurity expert. Explore, learn, and stay secure!
