# DCDC (Mock CCDC)

**DCDC** stands for **DSU Cyber Defense Competition**, and is our version of [CCDC](/competitions/external_competitions/ccdc/). Similar to CCDC, the competition is focused on improving the security of your given machines and services, while defending them from the [Red Team](/competitions/dsu_competitions/red_team/), and ensuring that the services stay available and functional. Additionally, you are tasked to respond effectively to various business tasks ([Injects](/resources/injects/)).

The [Scoring Engine](/competitions/dsu_competitions/scoring_engine/) is responsible for determining the uptime of your services, and will also handle inject submissions and red team points. If you change your passwords, you need to tell the Scoring Engine, since it is logging into your services (see [Scoring Engine: DCDC](/competitions/dsu_competitions/scoring_engine/#dcdc)).

For a place to start, see the sample [CCDC Checklist](/resources/ccdc-checklist/).
