# HiveCTF

HiveCTF is a (planned to be) yearly competition run by the CTF Club and developed by its leaders and members. It is a beginner-friendly CTF covering all the standard categories, but we like to spice it up with unique challenges.

## Want to help?

We'll take all the help we can get! We mainly need people to make and test challenges. It doesn't matter your skill level--come with a unique idea and we'll help you make it real. Join the [Computer Club](https://discord.defsec.club) Discord server and select the HiveCTF role to stay up to date and get involved.

## Past competitions

- **2026**: The first ever HiveCTF competition was held on April 18. We attracted 200 attendees and distributed over $4,000 worth of prizes, courtesy of MetaCTF and the Computer Club. <https://ctftime.org/event/3233/>
