# Injects

Injects, or "business tasks", or "anomalies", are tasks given to competing teams that ask them, typically, to solve some challenge, to answer some business-related question, to provide information on their network, or to modify the functionality of their systems.

The only accepted format for injects is **PDF**. Even if the inject asks for a picture or something similar, wrap it in a PDF, and try to make it look nice.

Injects can be submitted via the inject portal on the [Scoring Engine](/competitions/dsu_competitions/scoring_engine/). Late injects will be worth 50% of the original points.
