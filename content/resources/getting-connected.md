# Connecting to the Competition Environment

When competing in one of our events or participating in a lab, you have the choice of connecting via [OpenVPN](/resources/vpn/) or using the [vCloud interface](/resources/ialab/).

You can use the "physical access console" through vCloud, which is useful for graphical environments (Windows), or if you lock yourself out over the network.

## Competing

The VPN files for a competition will be sent in [Discord](https://discord.defsec.club) before a competition begins. We will also provide credentials to the [IALab](/resources/ialab/) around the same time.
